Subject Line Assist

Increasing AI adoption 8× by redesigning a reactive modal into proactive inline autocomplete.

CompanyKlaviyo
FocusInteraction Design · Experimentation
Impact8× account-level adoption
Subject Line Assist cover

Background

AI capability without usability

Marketers need to write subject lines that drive engagement — but Klaviyo's Subject Line Assistant was used in only ~1.7% of messages. The AI was capable; the interaction design was the bottleneck.

  • AI investment wasn't translating into usage or value
  • SLA lived in a multi-step modal disconnected from the writing flow
  • Required heavy prompting and multiple clicks to access
  • Output felt generic — users lacked trust and rarely returned

Design thinking

Guidance without friction

My goal was to redesign Subject Line Assist to reduce cognitive load, improve relevance, and increase adoption toward a ~5% target. I explored three concept directions before converging.

Keyword-Based Prompting
Allowed users to guide AI with keywords, but created uncertainty around intent and accuracy.
Toolbar-Based Controls
More efficient but still required explicit activation and added UI complexity.
Performance-Informed
Surfaced past subject lines with open rates, increasing trust but raising data clarity concerns.

Key insight

Users wanted flexibility and relevance but were unwilling to take extra steps to access AI. Guiding principle: guidance without friction.

Experimentation

Three experiments, one clear winner

I designed and shipped a set of experiments to isolate the impact of proactive vs. reactive AI and inline vs. modal interactions.

Experimentation takeaway

Proactive inline AI clearly outperformed reactive and modal patterns — driving ~6× higher message-level adoption and ~8× higher account-level adoption. Interaction design, not AI capability, was the constraint.

Final design

Proactive adaptive autocomplete

The winning solution was proactive, adaptive autocomplete embedded directly into the subject line field.

Final Design

Key product & design decisions

Discoverability

AI suggestions appear inline when the field is focused — no button, no modal, no extra step.

Timing

Suggestions refresh after ~3 words and a short pause — responsive without being distracting.

Control

Clear loading and cancel states let users pause or dismiss at any moment — AI supports, never blocks.

Exploration

Regenerate enables trying different directions without starting over — encouraging iteration, not commitment.

Graceful fallback

When brand data is unavailable, the system falls back gracefully — no broken states, no dead ends.

Impact

Results

~1.5% → ~8–9%
Message-level adoption (~6× increase)
~3% → ~24%
Account-level adoption (~8× increase)

My key contributions

  • Moved Subject Line Assist to proactive inline autocomplete — replacing reactive and modal patterns that capped adoption
  • Partnered on experiments that isolated interaction models, unlocking ~6× message-level and ~8× account-level adoption
  • Tuned suggestion timing, loading, cancel, and fallback paths so AI supports — never blocks — manual input
  • Validated the pattern as scalable across Klaviyo
