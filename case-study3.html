Case study · Athenic AI

Data XRay

Athenic lets business teams analyze data in plain English — no SQL, no data team required. I led the end-to-end design to make that promise feel real.

Data XRay cover
Company

Background

Powerful AI. But nobody could use it.

Users would sign up, get confused, and leave before experiencing any value. Not because the AI failed — because the experience got in the way.

Unclear question asking
Users didn't know how to phrase questions the AI could actually answer.
Hidden product value
Users couldn't see enough of the product to understand what was possible.
No continuity
Every session felt like starting over — no history, no follow-ups, no flow.
Fragmented flows
Dashboard and search were disconnected — constant context switching.
Understanding pain points

Design thinking

Fewer features. More clarity.

Rather than adding more AI power, I focused on instructional design, discoverability, and conversational continuity. I led a whiteboard session with engineering to map capabilities and prioritize what to build first.

Key insight
Users didn't need more analytical power — they needed clarity, guidance, and continuity. The constraint wasn't the AI. It was the experience around it.

Final design

Ask. See. Remember.

Chat, charts, and saved insights unified in one workbook. Users ask a question, see the answer visualized, and build on it — without ever losing their place.

Reflection
Powerful AI is only valuable when users understand and trust it. By reframing analytics as a conversational, insight-driven experience, Data XRay helped non-technical teams engage with their data confidently — without needing a data analyst in the room.
