Background

Analytics tools that exclude their users

Business teams increasingly rely on data to drive growth, but analytics tools remain complex — requiring technical setup, SQL knowledge, and precise querying. Despite strong underlying capabilities, many users struggled to activate and retain value from Athenic AI.

  • Low activation and retention due to complex analytics workflows
  • Users didn't know how to ask effective questions
  • AI responses felt disconnected from visible product value
  • Users rarely asked follow-up questions or reused insights

Design thinking

Make insights discoverable, conversational, reusable

Rather than adding more features, I focused on instructional design, discoverability, and conversational interaction. I started by mapping user tasks using session recordings and qualitative feedback.

Understanding pain points
Unclear question asking
Users submitted vague or unrelated queries, unsure how to phrase effective questions.
Hidden product value
Users couldn't experience enough features to understand the product's full value.
No continuity
Follow-up questions and insight reuse were rare due to disconnected dashboards.
Fragmented flows
Features were split across dashboards and search flows with no unified experience.

I led a whiteboard session with engineering to map essential capabilities and prioritize what to build.

Whiteboard session

Over 12 rounds of user testing, I continuously redesigned to reduce friction, improve clarity, and drive sustained engagement.

Iteration studies

Key insight

Users didn't need more analytical power — they needed clarity, guidance, and continuity.

Final design

A unified workbook for conversational analytics

The final design merged chat, charts, and saved insights into a single workbook — making insights feel like a conversation, not a query.
